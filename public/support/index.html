Support

Quick answers for the common questions — and a human if you need one.

How do I save a recipe from Instagram or Pinterest?

Tap the post's Share button and choose Dilla. You stay where you are — Dilla imports in the background and notifies you when the recipe's in your library. If the recipe is only in the video, or behind a "link in bio", Dilla fetches that too.

I can't find Dilla in the share sheet

iOS hides new apps at the end of the share row. Tap Share, scroll the app row to the end, tap More, then drag Dilla to the top (or tap Edit and mark it a Favorite).

A post won't import

Private and age-restricted posts can't be read by any app. The fix that always works: screenshot the recipe and share the image to Dilla — it reads screenshots, photos of cookbooks, and handwritten cards too.

How many recipes can I save?

Importing is limited per month (your count and its reset date are in Settings). Recipes already in your library never expire and are always available.

I forgot my password

On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot your password? and we'll email you a reset link.

How do I delete my account?

Settings → Delete account permanently erases your account and all its data, immediately. See the privacy policy for exactly what's stored.
